TracePhysique Support

TracePhysique is an app by MORDOBO TECHNOLOGIES LLC.

Write to us

Have a question, is something not working as expected, or do you want to tell us what you think of the app? Write to support@mordobo.com, in English or Spanish.

To help you sooner, tell us what happened, your phone model and the app version (Profile › Settings › About › Version). You do not need to send us your health answers or your exported files.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. TracePhysique works without an account and offline: your data is stored on your phone and, if turned on, in its backup. On iPhone, that backup does not include your health data.

How do I export my data?

Once you finish the initial questionnaire, go to Profile › "Export my data". The app prepares a JSON file with all your data and a CSV file with your sets, and opens your phone's share sheet twice, first for the JSON and then for the CSV: save them (on iPhone, in the Files app) or send them wherever you want.

How do I delete my data on iPhone?

  • From the app: Profile › "Delete account" deletes everything stored on the phone: your profile, your program and your whole history. There is no account in this version: the option deletes the data on the device.
  • If you have not finished the initial questionnaire, delete the app: touch and hold its icon › "Remove App" › "Delete App", or go to Settings › General › iPhone Storage › TracePhysique › "Delete App" (names may vary by iOS version). "Offload App" is not enough: it removes the app but keeps its data.
  • The backup: deleting your data in the app does not instantly delete a backup that already exists. You can manage or delete it in Settings › your name › iCloud.

And on Android?

  • From the app: Profile › "Delete account", as on iPhone.
  • If you have not finished the initial questionnaire, uninstall the app or go to Android Settings › Apps › TracePhysique › Storage › Clear storage (names may vary by manufacturer).
  • The backup: Google's backup may keep the app's data, and if you reinstall the app, Android may restore it from that backup. You can manage or delete it from your Google account settings.

How do I withdraw my health consent?

In Profile › Settings › Privacy and consents › "Withdraw consent". Your health answers, your pain reports, the exercise swaps you made because of discomfort and your program are deleted; the rest of your training history is kept.

Health disclaimer

TracePhysique is not a medical device and its content is not medical advice: it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. If you have a health condition, an injury or any concerns, talk to a health professional before you start.

If you feel pain during a session, tap "Pain": the app pauses the exercise and lets you swap it, end the session, or carry on if it was mild discomfort. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Privacy

What data the app uses, where it is stored and how to exercise your rights is in the privacy policy. For a privacy request, write to the contact in its section 11.
